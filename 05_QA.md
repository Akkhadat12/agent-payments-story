## 1 Evidence and factual integrity

Recheck source dates and time-sensitive claims at publication. Compare every label, number, voice claim and visual implication with 02's ledger and current source. Do not read geometric size, animated speed or a progress bar as neutral decoration if viewers could interpret it as measured.

- S00/S01: THB 3,000 is explicitly hypothetical total budget; no actual price, real purchase or Thailand availability inference
- S02: mandates show authorization evidence, not guaranteed correct choices, legal immunity or a universal mandatory architecture
- S03: keep OpenAI's March 2026 change and Google Sep2026 cart-transfer coexistence; source image is labeled published May2026. Mastercard example requires explicit consumer approval. Fail an all-autonomous interpretation
- S04: rates belong to the right service and unit, with date and provider context. No implied paid test, provider revenue or autonomous GPU procurement. Cards/stablecoins must not be treated as mutually exclusive universes
- S05: only the data-fee arithmetic 10 × $0.10 = $1 is sourced. Other costs are unquantified. Fail fake cost shares or “$1 finished report”
- S06: exact source denominator and dataset scope. 0.6–7.5% means two heuristic estimates of screened $25.62m, not confidence interval/global share; excluded activity is not labeled fake. Compare [TRM JSON](references/trm-x402-measurement.json)
- S07: old-version research and disclosed mitigation are not evidence of current vulnerable vendors. No guarantee of automated refund or definitive liability assignment
- S08: conditional thesis, no guaranteed savings, market-size projection or inevitable winner

For any correction that changes spoken factual wording, stop dependent signoff and set `BLOCKED`, `Next: Research/Story — revise narration script`, with exact scene, claim and evidence. Build may not silently repair substantive research prose.

## 4 Scene composition and clipping acceptance

Capture cover and every settled scene at the primary viewport, plus representative transition frames. At normal viewing scale run a first-glance focal test before reading the brief: what draws attention first, and is it the intended relationship? Inspect alignment, spacing, scale, margins, balance, visual weight, z-order, subject proportions, label proximity, connector endpoints, accidental tangencies and safe-area use. A successful build is not a substitute for this review.

Scene-specific checks and failure conditions:

- **S00:** screenshot first public frame after loading. Shoe/authorization relationship is spatially legible, title secondary, target visible. Fail a title-only cover, missing depth, clipped shoe or target, or automatic advancement
- **S01:** check budget beside its purchase and visible conditions. Fail hidden scenario qualifier, budget detached from total cost, constraints colliding with shoe or an unexplained brand implying a real order
- **S02:** all three functional roles visible and endpoints attached. Fail permission bypass implied by path, clipped control stage, ambiguous direction or roles collapsed into decorative nodes
- **S03:** inspect local source-image derivative at actual placement and in motion. Both relevant checkout paths, actual identity and publication qualifier remain legible. Fail unintended cropping, stretching, fake interface reconstruction, text too small to identify the choices, or receipt target mistaken for a real checkout action. Documentary text exception must be documented
- **S04:** service units stay near the right objects; task output is distinct. Fail wrong provider/rate pairing, a $/hour versus $/query magnitude chart, collision, unverified data-center/GPU implication or all inputs obscured by labels
- **S05:** arithmetic and “Data only”/“Illustrative” remain visible. Fail crop/overlap, invented numeric segments for retry/review, or an output treated as useful without a visible quality distinction
- **S06:** see denominator and uncertainty without help panel. Fail a misleading pie, invented proportional geometry, absent screened-base qualifier, range labeled certainty, or essential numbers clipped at any test size
- **S07:** paid and delivered are distinct states; unresolved delivery remains unresolved. Fail visual completion despite failed fulfillment, fake current breach allegation, guaranteed-refund symbolism or a giant generic lock replacing the explanation
- **S08:** both A and B remain recognizable with shared owner boundaries/outcome. Fail a simple repeat of cover, hidden conclusion questions, winner-logo wall or inevitable-future imagery

Resize at least S00/S03/S06/S08 during or after navigation; inspect all other settled states at the secondary viewport. Essential details may not disappear to “make it fit.” An intentional crop passes only with recorded reason, retained identity/claim and a deliberate look. Material clipping, off-frame control, text collision, image distortion or broken hierarchy is FAIL. Require crop/layout correction, a new public deployment when needed, fresh evidence and independent retest.
